About Mountain Hiking

Hiking is one of the best ways to experience the great outdoors. Weather your a beginner or an experienced trekker, theres a trail for everyone. From gentle forest walks to challenging mountain summits, each hike offers unique views, fresh air, and a chance to disconnect from the busy world.

The Atlantic region offers some of the most diverse hiking opportunities, from coastal cliffs to mountain peaks. Pack your gear, lace up your boots, and let's explore!

Essential Hiking Gear

  • 🎒 Comfortable hiking backpack (20-30L capacity)
  • 👟 Proper hiking boots with ankle support
  • 💧 Water bottles or hydration pack (2L minimum)
  • 🗺️ Trail map and compass/GPS device
  • 🧥 Weather-appropriate layers and rain jacket
  • 🍎 High-energy snacks and trail mix
  • 🔦 Headlamp or flashlight with extra batteries
  • 🩹 First aid kit with bandages and pain relief
  • 🧴 Sunscreen and insect repellent
  • 📱 Fully charged phone with emergency contacts
