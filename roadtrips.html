🗺️ Famous Road Trip Routes

  1. Pacific Coast Highway (California)

    Experience stunning ocean views from San Francisco to San Diego. 650 miles of coastal beauty.

  2. Route 66 (Illinois to California)

    The iconic "Mother Road" showcasing classic Americana across 2,448 miles.

  3. Blue Ridge Parkway (Virginia to North Carolina)

    469 miles of mountain scenery, perfect for fall foliage viewing.

  4. Overseas Highway (Florida Keys)

    Drive across 42 bridges connecting islands, ending at Key West. 113 miles of tropical paradise.

  5. Going-to-the-Sun Road (Montana)

    Glacier National Park's 50-mile scenic drive through spectacular mountain terrain.
